## Fixed scope and status meanings

Keep 28 × 28 × 1.6 mm, **two copper layers**, 11HS12-0674S motor rated 0.67 A/phase, required buzzer, rear motor case-bolt axes at X/Y ±11.5 mm, and no custom carrier. The Ø2.7 mm holes and R2.35 mm head exclusions are provisional. The configured whole-assembly frame target is 28.3 × 28.3 mm, not an allowance to enlarge the PCB.

Only untouched supported JLCPCB imports are allowed. Do not hand-edit supplier component definitions, pin maps, symbols, footprints or CAD; do not patch native circuit JSON. NEMA14's corrected source-library definitions, slot enlargement, artwork handling or supplier models cannot be copied as a workaround to this policy.

**Retained source** means present in the inspected declarative source/configuration, not proven native connectivity, correct footprint, fit, or functioning hardware. **Missing feature** requires design work. **Mechanical adaptation** needs NEMA11-specific geometry and measurements. **Design choice** requires reconciliation with requirements. **Deferred manufacturing/bench** remains unaccepted until the stated evidence exists. Every row below remains open at full-board acceptance level.

## Tool issues: investigate without hiding board failures

The [2026-10-23 issue record](tscircuit-issues-20261023.md) is historical evidence to reproduce, not a current-version verdict. Current package pins tscircuit 0.0.3121 and CLI 0.1.2446; validate actual installed transitive versions and lockfile separately.

1. **USB4145 locator mismatch (T01):** required one Ø0.71 mm round locator and one 1.01 × 0.71 mm slot, 8 mm apart (manufacturer drawing tolerance ±0.05 mm). Older imports had a pinched undersized slot; a later historical import emitted about 1.51 × 0.71 mm. Its dimensional disagreement remains unresolved. Raw supplier fetch previously returned 403, so do not assert importer-only responsibility. Exclude C5815189 until latest supported native import passes drawing/rotation/model/CAM review. Do not patch it or copy NEMA14's patched assets.
2. **Multi-sheet export (T02):** default export previously emitted first sheet only. Use supported per-sheet rendering and verify complete current component coverage. The refactored source requires seven pages; old six-page PDFs are historical.
3. **Imported internal labels (T03):** TMP102/RGB defaults historically violated internal box-label clearances. Reproduce on exact current imports. Board symbol placement may be adjusted legitimately, but imported symbol internals, names and pin semantics must not be suppressed or hand-corrected.
4. **Checker exit semantics (T04):** exit 0 may contain findings. Fail closed on findings, truncated/unparseable/incomplete reports and missing output. Exit code alone is not a pass.
5. **Upgrade exit semantics (T05):** historical child installer failures could be swallowed. Record installed versions and successful resolution/integrity, not only upgrade command status.
6. **Connectivity metadata (T06):** direct net labels historically disagreed with `is_connected`. Compare actual source net membership, emitted pin and label/wire geometry, and physical routing when permitted. Never edit native JSON flags to manufacture a pass.
7. **Import semantics/access (T07):** generic chip wrappers, missing pinAttributes/reference roles or insertionDirection are unresolved import/assembly checks. Drawings/models/plug access must settle physical direction; do not silence connector warnings by fabricated metadata. Warnings do not alone prove hardware is wrong or that upstream is at fault.
8. **Oval rotation/native-CAM consistency:** carry over the NEMA14 repro question, not its claimed fix. Reproduce with supported exact imported connector geometry and native/export output before attributing or resolving it.

Fresh source/placement failures remain design failures unless isolated evidence shows otherwise. No claim is made here that an upstream issue was posted, a dependency patch released or a tool defect repaired.

## Verification order and stop conditions

1. Freeze the candidate inputs and record source/config/import/model/lockfile hashes. Reconcile remaining design choices and preserve no-routing. Candidate expectation is 99 source components/89 fitted; side counts are placement-dependent, not accepted constants.
2. Verify untouched import provenance, physical manufacturer pin nets, all values/features and DNP semantics. Fresh format/typecheck/structural tests and all five native source checks (netlist, pin specification, source, schematic placement, PCB placement) are required. Run serially with stale-output removal, complete logs and strict parsers.
3. Independently inspect newly emitted supplier identities/pins, passives, board outline/layers, all pages, population and **zero traces/vias** while routing is disabled. A successful native build is only evidence that it built, not acceptance of layout or hardware.
4. Close mechanical/import/placement/schematic/thermal/stackup gates and design-choice gaps before enabling routing. No skipped feature, default warning suppression, invented geometry, or changed motor/board identity to make a checker green.
5. Only after authorized routing: independent copper connectivity/short/clearance/current/USB/oscillator audits and native/CAM agreement. Manufacturing and bench gates remain separate. Explicit publication/order approval is still required even after engineering checks pass.
